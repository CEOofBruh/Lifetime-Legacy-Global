Every associate in Lifetime Legacy Global is integrated into a multi-dimensional matrix. To initialize your first downline placement credit, you must calculate your regional matrix voucher using standard geometric laws.

[REGIONAL PROMO CODE GENERATION LAWS]

LAW 1: Identify the total number of Vertices ($V$) on a 4-node Tetrahedron Matrix.

LAW 2: Multiply $V$ by the total number of Faces ($F$) on the solid.

LAW 3: Subtract the total number of Edges ($E$) from the result.

LAW 4: Multiply the final integer by 131.7 and truncate all remaining decimals.
